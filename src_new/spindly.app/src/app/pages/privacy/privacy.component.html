Spindly ("Spindly", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it when you use our website at spindly.app and the Spindly label management platform (collectively, the "Service").

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, this policy also describes how we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are located in the Philippines, it describes how we comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. Who is the data controller?

Spindly is the data controller for personal information collected through spindly.app and the Spindly platform. If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise your data rights, contact us at hi@melt-records.com.

Labels that use the Spindly platform to manage their artists are independent data controllers (or, in some jurisdictions, joint controllers) for the personal data of their artists, staff, ticket buyers, and donors that they upload or collect through the platform. This policy covers Spindly's own data practices, not those of individual labels.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you give us directly

  • Contact form (spindly.app/contact): When you submit an inquiry, we collect your full name, email address, contact number (optional), label name (optional), and a free-text description of your label.
  • Account registration: Label admins are onboarded by invitation. During registration we collect your email address, chosen username, first and last name, and password (stored as a one-way bcrypt hash). You may also add a profile photo.
  • Platform content: Any artist profiles, release metadata, financial records, audio files, photos, documents, and contracts you upload through the platform.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Login activity: When you sign in, we log your IP address, proxy IP (if applicable), the authentication method used (password or Google), timestamp, and associated brand and user ID. This is used for security monitoring and fraud prevention.
  • Session tokens: After login, we issue a JSON Web Token (JWT) that is stored in your browser's local storage. We do not use HTTP cookies for session management.
  • Google Fonts: Our website loads fonts from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com). This causes your browser to send your IP address to Google as part of the font request. Google's privacy policy governs this data transfer.

c. Information from third-party sign-in providers

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID from Google. We never receive your Google password.

4. Data processed on behalf of labels (label-controlled data)

When a label uses Spindly to manage artists, ticket buyers, or donors, it uploads or generates personal data about those individuals. In this context:

  • The label is the data controller for that data.
  • Spindly acts as a data processor on the label's behalf.
  • Labels are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect that data and for informing affected individuals about how it will be used.
  • If you are an artist, ticket buyer, or donor whose data appears in the platform as a result of a label's use of Spindly, please contact that label directly to exercise your data rights. You may also contact us at hi@melt-records.com and we will assist where we can.

5. AI-powered features

The platform includes optional AI-powered features such as AI-generated press releases and song summaries. When these features are used, we transmit text-based metadata to a third-party large language model (LLM) provider for processing — for example, a song title, genre, mood tags, lyrics, or a pitch description.

Audio files and images are never sent to third-party AI providers. Only text-based metadata and descriptions are used to prompt the AI. We do not share personal information about individual users beyond what is strictly necessary for the feature being used.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

a. Third-party service providers

To operate the Service, we engage third-party service providers for functions such as file storage, email delivery, payment processing, sign-in authentication, AI feature processing, and infrastructure. These providers are given access to your data only to the extent necessary to perform their functions and are not permitted to use it for other purposes.

We may update the specific providers we use from time to time without notice, provided the categories of processing remain the same. If you would like to know which providers we currently use, contact us at hi@melt-records.com.

b. Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Spindly, our users, or the public.

c. Business transfers

If Spindly is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

7. International data transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside the Philippines and the EEA. Where we transfer personal data to countries that are not deemed adequate by the European Commission, we rely on standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, or other appropriate safeguards. For transfers from the Philippines, we comply with the cross-border transfer rules of the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

8. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy. We do not automatically delete data on a fixed schedule — your data remains accessible to you for as long as you use the Service.

If you wish to have your personal data deleted, you may request this at any time by contacting us at hi@melt-records.com. We will action deletion requests promptly, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain certain records (for example, financial transaction records required under applicable tax law).

When a label account is terminated, we will retain the account data for a reasonable period to allow for any outstanding billing, disputes, or legal compliance obligations, after which it will be deleted.

9. Cookies and local storage

The Spindly platform does not use cookies for session management. Authentication tokens (JWTs) are stored in your browser's local storage, not in cookies. This means these tokens are not automatically sent with HTTP requests and are not subject to the EU Cookie Directive in the same way that cookies are.

However, our website does load fonts from Google's servers, which may result in Google setting cookies or receiving your IP address. You can prevent this by using a browser extension that blocks third-party requests.

We do not currently use any analytics tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or behavioural tracking cookies on spindly.app. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

10. Your rights

Under GDPR (EEA / UK users)

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction — ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority if you believe we have violated your rights.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Philippine users)

You have the right to:

  • Be informed — know how your data is being processed.
  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate, false, or incomplete data.
  • Erasure or blocking — ask us to erase or block data that is unlawfully obtained or no longer necessary.
  • Damages — be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, or unlawfully obtained personal data.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Lodge a complaint — with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at privacy.gov.ph.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hi@melt-records.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including bcrypt password hashing, HTTPS for all data in transit, and access controls on our storage and database systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by law.

12. Children's privacy

The Spindly platform is intended for use by businesses and is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page and, where the changes are material, notify registered users by email. Continued use of the Service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
